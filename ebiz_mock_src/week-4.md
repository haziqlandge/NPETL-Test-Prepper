Q14) A firm is deciding how far to pursue collaborative inventory practices for two products. Product X has a long shelf life and high value; product Y has a short selling period and low value. Which conclusion is most consistent with the lecture?
A) Product Y justifies the tightest collaborative inventory control, while product X does not

Q15) A retail chain notices that its factory receives order swings far larger than the actual customer sales swings. Which measure most directly targets the root cause of this amplification identified in the lecture?
